# Wait Watcher findings - August 2026

Comparing August 2026 with August 2025. Source: NHS England referral to treatment (RTT) statistics.
Within 18 weeks = share of people still waiting who have waited 18 weeks or less. Typical wait = interpolated median. Points = percentage points.

## England

- Waiting: 7,345,544 then, 7,189,213 now.
- Within 18 weeks: 60.9% then, 65.2% now (+4.2 points). The NHS aims for 92%.
- Typical wait: 13.5 weeks then, 12.2 weeks now.
- Over 52 weeks: 192,024 then, 112,958 now.
