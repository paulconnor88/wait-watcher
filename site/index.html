      About Wait Watcher

      What this is

      • Built on NHS England's monthly referral to treatment (RTT) statistics.
      • England only.
      • Updated monthly, about six weeks behind.
      • Private providers doing NHS work are included.
      • Hospital ratings come from the Care Quality Commission (CQC). Contains CQC information. Ratings can be several years old - check the inspection date.

      What it can't tell you

      • Your place in the queue, or whether your referral arrived.
      • Waits for an exact operation - the data is by treatment type.
      • Differences between sites within one trust.
      • Private-pay prices or waits.

      For your own referral, try the NHS App, Manage Your Referral, or the hospital's Patient Advice and Liaison Service (PALS).

      Wait Watcher shows what the published data says. It can't know your individual case - your GP and hospital can.

      Your privacy

      • We don't store your answers.
      • Feedback is anonymous unless you add an email.
      • Emails are only used to reply about your feedback, never for marketing.
